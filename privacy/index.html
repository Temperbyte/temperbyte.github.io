Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

The free calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into them is sent to us or stored by us.

What we collect

  • Calculators: nothing. The math happens on your device.
  • Email: if you write to temperbyte.help@gmail.com, we use your address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by our store platform. We receive your order details (what you bought, your email) so we can deliver and support the product. Card details never reach us.
  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Temperbyte Uploader (Google / YouTube)

Temperbyte Uploader is our own internal tool. It is used only to upload Temperbyte's own videos to Temperbyte's own YouTube channel. It does not access, store or share any other person's Google or YouTube data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. It also uses YouTube API Services, see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing temperbyte.help@gmail.com.
