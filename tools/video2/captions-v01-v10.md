## v4 – RPE Calculator (PT, hyökkäävä)
Your client did 100 kg × 5 at RPE 8. Don't test their max.

RPE 8 means about 2 reps left, so 5 reps at RPE 8 is roughly a 7-rep max: about 123 kg estimated 1RM. No max test needed.

The free RPE calculator gives the working weight for any reps and RPE, rounded to your plates (kg or lb).

## v8 – Months of inventory (välittäjät, slideshow)
Buyer's market or seller's market? Depends on the price band.

All price bands: 4.0 months, balanced. $600k and up: 7.8 months, a buyer's market. Same town, same month.

Months of inventory = active listings ÷ sales per month. Use your MLS's numbers; thresholds vary by market.
