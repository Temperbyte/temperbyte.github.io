For real estate agents

  • Comps price calculator

    Comparable sales in: price per square foot for each comp, outliers flagged, the median and a suggested list price range with the math shown.

  • Price reduction calculator

    Days on market, showings and feedback give a hold or discuss-price signal, and the search bracket math shows which buyers a new price would reach.

  • Appraisal gap calculator

    The appraisal came in low: the buyer's cash and the seller's price for gap coverage, the buyer covering it, a price drop or a split.

  • MLS remarks checker

    Character count against your MLS limit, and every phrase that can cause fair housing trouble, with what to write instead.

  • Seller net sheet calculator

    Sale price, payoffs, commission, closing costs and credits in: the seller's estimated net, line by line, and the net at other prices.

  • Sale-to-list ratio calculator

    Paste recent sales with their list prices: each ratio, the median, how many sold over or under list, and the ratio to the original price.

  • Months of inventory calculator

    Active listings and recent sales in: months of supply, the absorption rate and whether the market leans toward buyers or sellers, for two areas or price bands.

  • Escalation clause calculator

    Every offer's final price after escalation, including two clauses competing against each other, and the seller's estimated net.

For fitness coaches

  • Progressive overload calculator

    Last session's sets in, next session's weight and rep targets out, with double progression and load jumps kept within about 5%.

  • Training volume calculator

    A program in, weekly hard sets per muscle group out, compared with common ranges for beginners, intermediates and advanced lifters.

  • RPE calculator

    Reps and RPE (reps in reserve) in: percent of 1RM, an estimated max and the working weight for the next session, rounded to your plates.

  • Client capacity calculator

    Your coaching hours and the minutes each client takes in: how many clients you can take, your load, and what faster check-ins free up.

  • Client adherence calculator

    Planned vs. done for sessions, nutrition, steps and check-ins, with a hold, keep going or one-small-change call.

  • Weight loss rate calculator

    Start weight, current weight and weeks in: the rate as a percent of body weight per week, and weeks to a goal weight at 0.5–1% a week.

  • Weight trend calculator

    For coaches and clients. Daily weigh-ins in, weekly averages, the rate per week and a 7-day trend chart out.
